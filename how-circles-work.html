Circle Information:

Every STAR program now runs as a "Circle": a self-managed cohort where members collaborate on cross-border research, training, or mentorship. These are led by Certified STAR Circle Leaders without needing constant oversight, empowering members to take full ownership of their work.

Register on our platform, browse the circle page and click on "Enroll Now" button to join. You'll need an approval from the Circle Leader to finalize the enrollment onto that circle. Circle Leaders will browse your STAR Platform profile and then notify you about your selection on that circle.

Circle FAQs:

1
Who can join? STAR Circles can be joined by anyone as long as they're approved by the Circle Leader and are registered on our STAR Platform. Registration is simple and takes less than 5 minutes. Each STAR Circle Leader determines your eligibility for joining that circle.
2
What is the Time Duration? STAR Circles run in a fixed schedule and time. They can range from weekly to monthly basis. The Circle Leader determines the duration and meeting times of each Circle (virtually or on site).
3
How many members can join a Circle? STAR Circles will have a fixed amount of people in it. The number of individuals that can join is determined by the Circle Leader as well. Also, some STAR Circles will be specific to particular individuals or region/country.
4
What is the medium of communication? STAR Circles will utilize our STAR Platform for onboarding, operation and for communication. However, for assignments and virtual meetings, Circle leaders will use their own preferred way or tool accordingly.

Circle Leader:

A circle Leader is an expert, star leader or scholar, who is resposible for the smooth running of any STAR Circle. A circle leader manages the cohort where members collaborate on cross-border research, training, or mentorship.

How do I become a Circle Leader?

  • 1.
    Application/Nomination: You can go to this page and fill out the form to apply as a Circle Leader. You may also nominate someone else as a Circle Leader.
  • 2.
    Eligibility for a Circle Leader: You don't need special requirements to become a Circle Leader. However, you need to be an expert in your respective field (determined by academic qualifications & contributions to the STAR community) to apply. Anyone affiliated to the STAR Community can nominate you as well.
  • 3.
    Steps to become a Circle Leader: Every Circle Leader should follow these processes to become a Circle Leader:
    1. They must create an account on the STAR Platform and register with correct information.
    2. They should be a Premium Member of the STAR Scholars Network.
    3. They should apply by filling out the Circle Leader application form.
    4. After applying, they should join the STAR Circle Leader orientation which informs them about the foundational steps to run a circle properly.
  • 4.
    After you become a Circle Leader: You can create a Circle according to your preferences on our STAR Platform. However, make sure that the Star Circle should be based on one of the STAR Programs.

As a circle leader, ensure the following requirements:

  • 1.
    A Circle Leader is responsible for determining the dates and duration of time for any circle.
  • 2.
    The number of individuals that can join the circle is determined by the Circle Leader at the beginning.
  • 3.
    A Circle Leader is responsible for the approval, pending and rejection of Circle enrollment requests by individually reviewing each individual's profile.
  • 4.
    A Circle Leader may use his own video conferencing tools to moderate the Circle. However, to post announcements and resources, he should use the Circle page.
